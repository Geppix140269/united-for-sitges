Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026. This notice explains how the independent United for Sitges information website and moderated community handles personal data. The project is not part of Sitges Town Hall, any political party, or the official electoral authorities.

1. Who is responsible?

Controller: Giuseppe Funaro, acting as the independent individual organiser of United for Sitges.
Contact: hello@giuseppefunaro.com for privacy requests, moderation, or account deletion. Please do not send passwords or identity documents unless specifically necessary through an agreed secure channel.

Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are not displayed to community moderators. The website does not request voter ID, electoral register details, party affiliation, vote intention, identity documents or donations.

3. What other people can see

Visitors can see approved posts and replies, including the public display name and publication date. Email addresses, passwords and private member records are not displayed. Pending contributions are visible only to their authors and authorised moderators; reports are accessible only to their authors and moderators. Moderators may reject, hide or remove content according to the community rules.

4. Sensitive personal information and political views

The forum discusses civic life, but does not ask users to reveal political beliefs, voting choices, health, religious beliefs, ethnicity or other sensitive personal information. Please do not include these details about yourself or anyone else in posts. Civic discussion may indirectly suggest personal opinions. Contributions undergo moderation and can be removed on request. We do not use forum posts for political profiling, targeted political advertising or automated decisions.

5. Service providers and international access

The website uses Vercel for hosting, Supabase for authentication and database storage (dedicated project in the EU, Ireland), and Resend for transactional emails (sending configuration in the EU). The website also requests fonts from Google Fonts where supported. Providers may process technical data or provide support internationally, subject to their applicable privacy policies and data processing and transfer arrangements. You can request more information about relevant safeguards via the contact email above.

We do not sell personal information or use member email addresses for marketing. Registration confirmation and password recovery emails are service communications.

6. Storage and deletion

Account records are maintained while membership is active. Approved contributions remain available while published; members may request removal. Rejected or removed contributions and moderation reports are retained only as long as reasonably needed to handle moderation, legal, or security issues, and reviewed for deletion within six months after resolution unless a specific legal obligation or dispute requires longer preservation. Security and operational logs are kept under the applicable providers' retention practices. Backups may take additional time to expire under provider schedules.

To close an account, remove posts or request a copy of personal data, write to hello@giuseppefunaro.com. We will verify account ownership where necessary and respond within the GDPR time limits, ordinarily within one month. Deletion requests may be subject to narrow legal or security exceptions. You can also withdraw consent for publication of individual contributions; this will not make earlier lawful processing invalid.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or object to relevant processing, and withdraw consent for public contributions. Contact the controller using the email above. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

8. Browser storage and age policy

The information pages may store the selected language locally. Supabase authentication uses local browser storage to keep a user securely signed in until logout or session expiry. The site may use essential technical storage needed for security or access, but does not currently operate third-party advertising or profiling. We recommend signing out on shared devices.

The public community is designed for adults aged 18 or over. If we learn an account belongs to a minor, we may suspend it and arrange appropriate deletion. The information pages remain available without membership.

9. Community participation and contact

All new discussions and replies require moderator approval. Rules prohibit harassment, discrimination, threats, exposing other people's personal data, coordinated campaigning and spam. You can report a public post from within the forum after signing in, or by emailing the community contact. Moderation decisions may be reviewed on request.

Independent civic information only: United for Sitges does not administer voter registration. For official electoral matters consult the Spanish Electoral Census Office (INE).

This policy may be updated to reflect new functionality or legal requirements. Material changes will be communicated on the site.
