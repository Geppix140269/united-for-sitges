## 5. Open issues and leads (need follow-up)

1. **ERC 2023 programme**: open `https://locals.esquerra.cat/documents/programa-erc-sitges-2023-27_textrefos_v2.pdf` in a normal browser. If it is the 2023-2027 programme, catalogue it, extract proposals and change `programme_2023_status` to `verified`. This is the single biggest coverage gain available.
2. **Junts, PSC, Verds en Comú Podem programmes**: press reports describe full programmes (VECPS: more than 1,000 points). Ask the parties for the documents or check their social media archives from May 2023.
3. **L'Eco de Sitges** campaign articles (2023): not reachable from the research environment. Worth checking manually as additional secondary sources.
4. **2023 government agreement**: signed 16 June 2023 by ERC, Sitges GI, Verds en Comú Podem and Fets per Sitges. No published text was found. If the council or parties publish it, catalogue it as `government_agreement` (separate evidence type).
5. **Municipal action plan**: no Pla d'Actuació Municipal 2023-2027 was found. The government presented a mid-term plan called "Sitges es mou" (34 actions) on 6 May 2025 ([Ràdio Maricel](https://www.radiomaricel.cat/el-govern-de-sitges-presenta-la-campanya-sitges-es-mou-un-pla-de-mandat-de-mitja-legislatura/)); no document located.
6. **Council motions and votes** (future evidence type): motion texts are published at https://www.sitges.cat/portal-de-govern-obert/mocions-del-ple-municipal (last two years, no votes); plenary videos at https://actes.sitges.cat/session/portadaPublica; minutes on seu-e.cat. Not used in this release.
7. **sitgesgi.com** labels its programme page "Programa electoral 2025" but links the 2023 files. Treated as a labelling error; the PDFs are dated May 2023.
8. **Translations**: proposal summaries are English only. Interface text is in 7 languages.
