Editorial transparency

How this comparison works

Independent and nonpartisan

United for Sitges is not standing in any election and does not support any party. This page does not judge which party is better and gives no voting recommendation.

Same rules for everyone

Every organisation is reviewed with the same 16 subject categories and the same layout. No party receives scores, colours, rankings or extra space. Default order is alphabetical.

Sources first

Each proposal is taken from a documented source, linked with its page or section. Summaries are our own editorial descriptions, not official party texts or translations. Short original excerpts are shown so you can check the wording.

2023 and 2027 are kept apart

2023 programmes are historical. 2027 commitments are added only when a party's 2027 programme or a verified public statement is published, and are always labelled with their year and source type.

Missing is not opposing

If we show no proposal for a party on a subject, it means we have not found or recorded one. It does not mean the party has no policy or opposes the idea.

Selection and limits

Programmes are long. We record a representative selection of concrete proposals per subject, favouring specific, checkable measures. The coverage table shows exactly what has been recorded. Open the source for everything else.

Corrections

Spotted an error or a new programme?

Parties, candidates and residents can send corrections or newly published documents. Please include a link to a verifiable source (an official programme, website or publication). We review every request and record changes in the public research log. Corrections without a verifiable source cannot be published.
