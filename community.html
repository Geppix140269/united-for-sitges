Community rules

1. Respect neighbours.
No insults, harassment, discrimination or threats.
2. Cite official sources.
Especially for election rights, registration and deadlines. Mark unverified claims clearly.
3. No campaigning or spam.
No party advertisements, coordinated promotion or candidate campaigning in general discussion.
4. Equal treatment.
Any future candidate Q&A must use published, equal and moderated rules for all participating parties.
5. Privacy matters.
Do not post another person's private details. All public posts are approved before publication.
