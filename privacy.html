United for Sitges

Privacy & community status

Public registrations are not open. United for Sitges currently offers independent civic information and a read-only community preview. Only the designated administrator can test a private moderator account. The community is not yet accepting public member registrations.

What is processed now?

The information homepage stores your selected language in your browser. The website is hosted on Vercel. The dedicated moderator onboarding area may process the designated administrator's email address, chosen display name, password credentials managed by Supabase Auth, session and necessary account records. Test discussion content may also be stored in a dedicated Supabase database hosted in the EU.

The password is managed by the authentication provider; it is not collected by the United for Sitges discussion pages as plaintext. Transactional email is prepared through Resend. Public forum membership is not yet active.

Project contact

For privacy, site management and moderation-related questions contact hello@giuseppefunaro.com.

Before the community opens

A fuller GDPR notice and community rules must be finalised, including confirmed controller details and legal basis, categories of user data, retention and deletion periods, recipients and processing locations, international transfers where applicable, member rights and a clear content reporting process. The site will not deliberately request political opinions or voting preferences. Public discussions about local issues may nevertheless contain sensitive personal information, so users will be reminded not to disclose unnecessary private details.

Moderation rules will prohibit harassment, personal data disclosure, spam, coordinated campaigning and unsourced claims about electoral eligibility. Future candidate question sessions, if introduced, will be separately announced and conducted under equal access rules.

Electoral information

United for Sitges is not a public authority and does not register voters or collect electoral roll information. The Spanish Electoral Census Office (INE) is the official source for eligibility and registration.
