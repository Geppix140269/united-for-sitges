## 6. Conflicts between sources

Source difference noted on 22 November 2026: the 26 May 2023 media interview describes an Aiguadolç sports facility with underground parking. The official programme’s page 10 specifies conversion of the Aiguadolç field to multipurpose use without mentioning underground parking there; page 13 places underground parking at Can Robert. Both records are retained with their own sources and evidence labels, without combining them. Party statements about the 2026 breakdown of the government agreement differ between parties (ERC and Sitges GI versus Verds en Comú Podem). These are not used as evidence of programme positions and are not summarised on the page.
