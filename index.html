Voting rights

Who can vote in Sitges?

Being empadronado (registered at your local address) is important, but does not by itself guarantee voting rights. You must be 18 by election day, qualify by nationality and appear on the relevant electoral roll.

Choose an option to see the general rules. This tool does not confirm your individual eligibility.

How to register

How to get onto the correct electoral roll

1. Check your padrón

Confirm you are registered at your actual address with the Ajuntament de Sitges. This is not the same as electoral registration.

2. Identify the route

Spanish citizens: standard electoral census. EU citizens: permanent declaration to vote in Spain. Agreement-country citizens: application for the 2027 municipal elections.

3. Submit through official channels

Use the INE electronic office where available, the OCE letter, or ask your Ajuntament for the relevant form. Keep evidence of submission and check your census status.

4. Verify before election day

Only the official electoral census establishes your right to vote in this election. For personalised advice, contact the INE or your municipal office.

Questions

Questions residents often ask

Is empadronamiento enough to vote?

No. It records where you live. Municipal voting also requires an eligible nationality and inclusion in the appropriate electoral census.

Can British residents vote after Brexit?

Yes, if they meet the UK–Spain bilateral agreement's requirements and apply to the electoral census for the election. For 2027, the application window runs from 1 December 2026 to 15 January 2027.

Must an EU citizen declare their intention every election?

Normally no. An EU declaration remains valid while the person continues to meet the conditions; if you have never declared it, act by 30 December 2026.

Can I ask the community a question?

A moderated community discussion space is being considered, but it is not active. For now the site provides public FAQs and verified links. Questions sent to the site are not collected, and no individual assistance is promised.

Is United for Sitges running in 2027?

No. United for Sitges is not presenting candidates in the May 2027 municipal election. It is an independent resident-information project.
