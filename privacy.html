Privacy & community status

The forum is not active. As of 11 November 2026, this site does not accept member registration, messages, donations or voter data. The optional language setting on the information homepage is saved in the visitor's browser.

A public forum may contain opinions that qualify as special-category personal data under EU law. This page is not yet the privacy notice for an operational forum.
